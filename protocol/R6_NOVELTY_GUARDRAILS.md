# R6 Novelty Guardrails

## Claim boundary

The paper does not claim priority for:

- bilateral retinal classification;
- multi-image or multi-eye fusion;
- uncertainty estimation;
- probability calibration;
- selective prediction or risk–coverage analysis;
- confidence filtering;
- act/defer or defer-to-human policies;
- quality-aware retinal classification;
- retinal foundation models;
- inter-eye reproducibility;
- optic-disc asymmetry;
- AMD progression modeling.

The intended scientific contribution is the structured examination of how supplied four-state bilateral biology, eye-specific human quality annotations, signed prediction asymmetry, acquisition strata, and model loss relate to one another, followed by a distinct held-out test of whether true fellow-eye output improves prediction of own-eye loss beyond a flexible own-eye-logit baseline.

Candidate C is loss prediction. It does not change the disease probability. Any bilateral recalibration or late-fusion analysis is outside the confirmatory contribution.

## Permitted wording

- “we investigate”;
- “we test”;
- “we examine”;
- “the literature search did not identify an exact equivalent”;
- “incremental information under the frozen model and BRSET setting”;
- “retrospective mechanistic association.”

## Prohibited wording

- “first”;
- “novel” as a priority claim;
- “unprecedented”;
- “no previous study”;
- “high-impact”;
- “myth-busting”;
- generic superiority over bilateral fusion or selective-prediction work;
- causal “device effect”;
- automatic deployment availability of human quality labels;
- drusen as AMD or increased cup-disc finding as glaucoma.

## Closest-collision boundary

The R1–R3 verified ledger and the second Gemini audit identify close work in confidence-guided multi-image retinal fusion, bilateral anatomical representations, selective prediction, conformal defer policies, AMD prognosis, and inter-eye/image-quality consistency. Those spaces bound interpretation. No model-performance result can broaden the claim after the fact.

## Null boundary

A null reliability result is not automatically important, field-changing, or evidence that fellow-eye reliability is universally useless. Without a defensible equivalence margin, only the point estimate, cluster-aware interval, achieved precision, and conditional scope may be stated.

## Deployment boundary

The primary retrospective reliability baseline includes human `image_field` and `focus`. Deployment-oriented language requires the inference-valid core that omits those variables or a separately validated automated quality module. Four-state ground truth and labels are never deployment inputs.

## Literature update rule

Before manuscript submission, repeat the documented literature search. New exact collisions trigger the R4/R6 reformulation rule. Literature findings may narrow wording; they may not motivate holdout-driven estimator changes.
